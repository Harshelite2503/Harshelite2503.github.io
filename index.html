Quant · Market Microstructure 2026

Argus — real-time dark-pool & options-flow surveillance

A live market-microstructure engine that streams every S&P 500 trade over WebSockets, detects off-exchange (dark-pool) prints via FINRA TRF attribution, and classifies Intermarket Sweep Orders and block trades from exchange condition codes in real time. A pluggable strategy engine scans the tape for institutional footprints — $200k+ dark prints, sweeps, blocks, ITM put-sells — and pushes alerts to a dashboard that overlays notional-sized dark-pool bubbles on live candlesticks. Each feed worker runs its own asyncio event loop on a dedicated thread; a FastAPI backend fans updates out to the browser at a 1s cadence. The zero-I/O detection core benchmarks at 2.4M trades/s single-threaded (0.42 µs per trade) — roughly 20× headroom over peak US consolidated-tape rates — and parses OCC option symbols at 310k/s, across a 503-name S&P 500 universe.

  • Python
  • asyncio
  • WebSockets
  • FastAPI
  • Market Microstructure
  • Options Flow
